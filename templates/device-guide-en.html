PlantECG Fluorometer Device Guide

Function

PlantECG is a portable, non-invasive chlorophyll fluorescence measurement device. By analyzing the chlorophyll fluorescence response to light pulses, it enables rapid assessment of plant physiological status and detection of stress symptoms.

Technical Specifications

  • Measurement type: Non-invasive, contact-based
  • Measurement time: 1 second (chlorophyll fluorescence fast induction curve OJIP recording)
  • Excitation light source: Blue LED (430 nm)
  • Excitation light intensity (illuminator): approx. 4000 µmol photons/m²/s
  • Analyzed signal: Photosystem II (PSII) activity in chloroplasts
  • Operating modes: Single measurement or measurement series
  • Portability: Compact and lightweight design suitable for field and laboratory use
  • Communication: Wireless connection to a computer, smartphone, tablet, or other devices via WiFi (WiFi access point: 2.4 GHz)
  • Dimensions: 95 × 58 × 23 mm
  • Weight: 80 g
  • Detector: PIN photodiode with 690 nm band-pass filter
  • Electronics: 32-bit controller, 12-bit ADC
  • Battery: Lithium-polymer, 3.7 V, 1200 mAh
  • Charging: USB Type-C

Fluorometer Components

The device consists of the following parts:

  1. Power switch
  2. Measurement head
  3. Measurement button
  4. Display
  5. Plant physiological status indicator
  6. USB-C charging port
  7. Battery / charging indicator

Getting Started

What You Need

  • PlantECG fluorometer (main unit)
  • A PC, tablet, or smartphone with a web browser capable of connecting to the local WiFi generated by the fluorometer (SSID: FLUO + last two digits of device ID, IP address: 192.168.35.1, password: assigned individually to each device)
  • Plant samples for measurement
  • Dark adaptation chamber or light-shielding material (recommended)

Measurement Procedure

Step 1: Dark Adaptation

To obtain optimal measurement results, allow the leaf to undergo dark adaptation for 20–30 minutes before measurement. Dark adaptation restores the photosystems to their initial state and improves the reliability of the fluorescence signal. Use a dark adaptation chamber or cover the leaf surface with light-shielding material.

Step 2: Positioning on the Leaf

Carefully bring the measurement head into contact with the leaf surface. Ensure gentle but firm contact without applying excessive force that could damage the tissue.

Step 3: Performing the Measurement

Press the measurement button once to initiate the measurement. The device emits a light pulse and automatically records the fluorescence response. The measurement takes approximately 1 second. Do not move the measurement head or leaf during this time. Basic measurement results are displayed on the display.

Step 4: Retaining Curves on the Graph

If the graph displayed on the WiFi-connected device is accepted as valid, press the measurement button twice to retain it.

Step 5: Ending a Measurement Series

After retaining the final curve (by pressing the measurement button twice), press the button two additional times to hide the individual curves and display their average value along with the variability range (±1 standard deviation). The measurement data are then saved as an ojip.csv file in the non-volatile memory of the WiFi-connected device.

Measurement Principle and Parameters

OJIP Curve

The device records the O-J-I-P fluorescence induction curve, analyzing the photosynthetic response over time scales ranging from microseconds to milliseconds:

  • O (Origin): Initial fluorescence when all reaction centers are open
  • J (Jump): Rapid rise phase (1–2 ms)
  • I (Intermediate): Slower rise phase (30 ms)
  • P (Peak): Maximum fluorescence when all reaction centers are closed

Basic Parameters

  • Fo: Minimum fluorescence intensity (reference value)
  • Fm: Maximum fluorescence intensity
  • Fv/Fm: Maximum quantum yield, typically ranging from 0.75 to 0.85 in healthy plants, defined as (Fm − Fo) / Fm
  • PI(ABS): Performance Index indicating the efficiency of photochemical reactions

Analysis of Measurement Results

The recorded fluorescence induction curves (118 data points) are saved in CSV format. These files can be imported into the PlantECG Analyzer for parameter calculation and graphical visualization.

CSV File Format: The first row of each file contains time values (milliseconds), followed by fluorescence intensity values in subsequent rows. Multiple measurement results can be stored in a single file.

Environmental data recorded at the time of measurement (temperature, atmospheric pressure, and relative humidity) are stored at the end of the file.

Analysis Tool: The ojip.csv file saved during measurements can be opened in a spreadsheet application or transferred to plantecg.pl, where a dedicated application automatically calculates approximately 40 photosynthetic parameters based on OJIP analysis and generates an ojipres.csv results file.

Recommended Practices

  • Measurement consistency: Due to light-induced photo-adaptation, repeated measurements on the same plant should be performed on different leaf areas.
  • Time of day: For comparative studies, perform measurements at consistent times to minimize diurnal variation.
  • Replicates: Increasing the number of measurements across multiple leaves improves statistical reliability.
  • Number of measurements: At least 3–5 measurements per condition are recommended.

Device Operation

  • Preparation: The fluorometer is battery-powered. Fully charge it via the USB Type-C port before use. To access all functions, connect a browser-enabled device to the WiFi network generated by the fluorometer (SSID: FLUO + last two digits of device ID).
  • Power control: Use the sliding switch on the side of the device. When switched toward the measurement head, the battery is disconnected from the circuit. In this state, measurements can be performed using external power, but this mode is not suitable for portable or continuous operation. When switched toward the display side, the battery is connected, enabling several hundred consecutive measurements when fully charged.
  • Charging indicator: When the battery level is low and a cable is connected, the indicator flashes to indicate charging. When sufficiently charged, it lights for 30 seconds and then turns off.
  • Plant physiological status indicator: Measurement results are displayed on the screen. The indicator light above shows plant physiological status: green (good), yellow (warning), red (severe stress).
  • Measurement operation: Press the measurement button once to start a measurement. If the result is valid, press the button twice to add the fluorescence curve to the graph. Repeat to retain multiple curves. Press the button twice again to end the measurement series.
  • Saving results: After completing a measurement series, a file named ojip.csv is automatically saved to the WiFi-connected device.

Troubleshooting

Weak or Unstable Signal

If measured values are lower than expected or unstable, check the following:

  • Proper contact between the measurement head and the leaf surface
  • Cleanliness of the leaf surface
  • Sufficient dark adaptation
  • Adequate battery charge

Device Not Responding

  • Charge the device for several hours
  • Toggle the power switch to restart the device
  • Contact technical support if the issue persists

Safety and Maintenance

  • Light exposure: The device emits high-intensity light pulses. Do not direct the measurement head toward the eyes.
  • Cleaning: Clean the measurement head with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use strong solvents or abrasives.
  • Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Battery: Charge regularly and consult service personnel if battery performance significantly degrades.

Contact and Support

For assistance with measurements, device maintenance, or other technical support, please contact the manufacturer or authorized distributor. Additional information and application notes are available at plantecg.pl.
