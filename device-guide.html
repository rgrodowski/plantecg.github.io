PlantECG Fluorymeter Device Guide

Overview

The PlantECG fluorymeter is a portable, non-invasive device designed to measure chlorophyll fluorescence in plants. It provides rapid assessment of plant physiological status and stress detection by analyzing how plants emit fluorescence when exposed to light pulses.

Getting Started

What You Need

  • PlantECG fluorymeter unit
  • Device with browser to connect local WiFi named FLUO generated by fluorymeter at 192.168.35.1 address
  • Plant samples for measurement
  • Dark adaptation chamber or cloth (optional but recommended)

Measurement Procedure

Step 1: Dark Adaptation

For best results, allow the plant leaf to dark-adapt for 20-30 minutes before measurement. This resets the photosystem and provides a baseline measurement. You can use the proper dark adaptation chamber or simply cover the leaf area with a cloth.

Step 2: Position the Leaf

Carefully position the leaf clip on a leaf. Ensure good contact between the measurement head and the leaf surface. Avoid damaged or diseased areas.

Step 3: Perform Measurement

Press the measurement button. The device will emit a light pulse and record the fluorescence response. The measurement typically takes 1 seconds. Do not move the device or leaf during measurement.

Step 4: Record Data

The device will store the measurement data automatically. Note the environmental conditions and any observations about the plant's condition.

Understanding Your Measurements

OJIP Curves

The device measures the O-J-I-P fluorescence induction curve, which captures the photosynthetic response over microseconds to milliseconds:

  • O (Origin): Initial fluorescence when all reaction centers are open
  • J (Jump): Fast rise phase (1-2 ms)
  • I (Intermediate): Slower rise phase (30 ms)
  • P (Peak): Maximum fluorescence when all reaction centers are closed

Key Parameters

  • Fo: Minimum fluorescence yield (baseline)
  • Fm: Maximum fluorescence yield
  • Fv/Fm: Maximum quantum efficiency (typically 0.75-0.85 for healthy plants)
  • PI(ABS): Performance Index indicating overall photosynthetic vitality

Data Export and Analysis

The fluorymeter exports data as CSV files containing the complete fluorescence induction curve with 118 data points. These files can be uploaded to the PlantECG Analyzer for detailed parameter calculations and visualization.

CSV File Format: Each file contains time values (in milliseconds) in the first row, followed by fluorescence intensity values. Multiple measurements can be included in a single file for batch processing and averaging.

Best Practices

  • Operating conditions: The device is not resistant to water and high humidity
  • Consistency: Measure different leaf position and area for repeated measurements on the same plant
  • Time of Day: Perform measurements at consistent times to avoid diurnal variations
  • Environmental Conditions: Record temperature, humidity, and light conditions
  • Replicates: Take multiple measurements on different leaves for statistical reliability
  • Sample Size: For meaningful analysis, measure at least 3-5 leaves per plant condition

Troubleshooting

Weak or Inconsistent Signal

If measurements show unexpectedly low fluorescence values, check that:

  • The leaf is properly positioned in the measurement head
  • The leaf surface is clean and free of dust
  • The leaf has been properly dark-adapted
  • The battery level is sufficient

Device Not Responding

  • Charge battery for a few hours
  • Turning off the power is equivalent to reset procedure
  • Contact technical support if problems persist

Safety and Maintenance

  • Light Exposure: The device emits intense, focused light pulses. Do not look directly into the measurement head.
  • Cleaning: Use a soft, dry cloth to clean the measurement head lens. Avoid harsh chemicals.
  • Storage: Store in a cool, dry place. Keep the dark adaptation chamber in a protective case.
  • Battery: Charge regularly and replace when performance decreases.
